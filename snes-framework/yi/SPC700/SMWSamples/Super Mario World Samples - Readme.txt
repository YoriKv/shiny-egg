This is a collection of every sample found in Super Mario World. Most of the samples are very simple, though they can be used in various ways with the correct ADSR and tuning
(e.g. the woodblock is just a power snare at a high pitch whereas Pilotwings takes SMW's chello and uses it as kind of trumpet).


It should be noted that SMW intentionally detunes certain instruments due to the lack of a subtuning and uses semitone tuning (think of the h-command in AMK) to get the correct pitch.
This is rectified within the patterns file which otherwise play on a different note other than o4c:
Here is a list of every simple with the correct tuning values as well as how much they're detuned in semitones:
Glockenspiel.brr: $03 $00 (+7 semitones)
Acoustic Grand.brr: $02 $AC (+5 semitones)
Snare Drum.brr: $05 $57 (-7 semitones)
Bongo.brr: $05 $59 (+5 semitones)
Orchestra Hit.brr: $01 $E4 (-8 semitones)

Any other sample is played at the real note and their pitch is therefore real (though I tuned the bass downwards by 12 semitones or a whole octave in the example MML).

Keep in mind that Thunder.brr is a sound effect only sample, though with the right tuning (e.g. "Thunder.brr" $0E $16 $7F $0E $00, note o4e), you can use it as an improvised clap sample.
